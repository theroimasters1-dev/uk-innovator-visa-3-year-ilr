# Fast-Track 3-Year ILR (Settlement) Under UK Innovator Founder Visa 2026

The **UK Innovator Founder Visa** provides the fastest route to permanent residency (Indefinite Leave to Remain - ILR) available under the UK Immigration Rules, reducing the standard 5-year waiting period to just **3 years**.

---

## 🏆 The 7 Commercial Settlement Milestones (Meet Any 2)
To secure Indefinite Leave to Remain at Year 3, a founder must satisfy at least **two** of the following commercial criteria:
1. **Capital Expenditure:** At least £50,000 invested into the business and actively spent on advancing the commercial plan.
2. **Customer Doubling:** The customer base has at least doubled in the past 3 years and exceeds the average for comparable UK competitors.
3. **Research & Development:** The business has engaged in significant R&D activity and applied for intellectual property protection in the UK.
4. **Gross Revenue Milestone:** Generated a minimum annual gross revenue of **£1,000,000** in the final year before ILR application.
5. **Revenue & Export:** Generated at least £500,000 gross revenue with at least £100,000 resulting from international exports.
6. **Job Creation (10 Jobs):** Created the equivalent of at least 10 full-time jobs for resident UK workers (each lasting at least 12 months).
7. **High-Wage Job Creation (5 Jobs):** Created at least 5 full-time jobs for resident workers with an average salary of at least £25,000/year.
