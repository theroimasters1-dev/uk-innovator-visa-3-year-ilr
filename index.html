UK Innovator Founder Visa: 3-Year ILR Blueprint

Unlike conventional UK immigration categories requiring 5 years of continuous residence, the UK Innovator Founder Visa permits founders to achieve permanent settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain - ILR) in just 36 months.

The 7 Statutory Settlement Criteria

Founders must satisfy at least two of the following conditions:

  • £50,000 capital invested and actively utilized.
  • Gross annual revenue exceeding £1,000,000.
  • £500,000 revenue with at least £100,000 generated through international export.
  • 10 full-time jobs created for resident UK workers.
  • 5 high-wage jobs created with average salaries above £25,000.
